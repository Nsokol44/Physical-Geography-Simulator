Physical Geography sim

Direct-manipulation tool for exploring hurricane track, intensity, surge, wind damage, orographic precipitation, tornadoes and lightning over real US coastal elevation transects.

  • Steer the yellow track; intensity responds to SST + landfall + slider. Damage is stronger in the lower-right (SE) quadrant.
  • Scenarios load guided setups. Hazard selector + Place for lightning, tornado, wildfire, extreme rain, or fluvial flood seed.
  • Fluvial flooding interacts with hurricane rainfall on low ground. Drag the blue rain ring.
  • Overlays, Export log, and touch controls support classroom and research use.

All processes are pedagogically simplified proxies (see Table 1 in the accompanying method paper). Pair with instructor framing.
